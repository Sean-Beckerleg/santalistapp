Privacy Policy for Santa's List

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Santa's List ("the app") is a family app that lets a parent keep a fun "Naughty or Nice" deed list for their children. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server.

Information stored on your device

The following is saved only on your own device and is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • The children's names and the deeds you log for them.
  • Your parent PIN, kept in the device's secure storage.
  • Your reminder on/off preference.

Uninstalling the app or using "Forgot PIN" deletes this data.

Notifications

If you turn on Reminders, the app asks for notification permission. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your phone. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your phone's settings at any time.

Children's privacy

The app is designed to be used by a parent or guardian. Children's names are entered by the parent and stay on the parent's device. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Third parties

The app does not share data with third parties and contains no third-party advertising or analytics.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this location with a new date.
