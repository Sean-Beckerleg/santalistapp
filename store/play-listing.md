## Full description (max 4000)
Have your children been naughty? Or nice? Santa's Naughty or Nice List is here to help you keep track of every good deed or misstep this holiday season.

Log your children's good (and not-so-good) deeds all year long and watch the Deed Meter swing between Naughty and Nice. Each child gets their own list, complete with Santa holding the scroll!

FEATURES
- A Deed Meter for each child that shows Naughty or Nice at a glance
- Add deeds with points, and remove them if you change your mind
- Christmas countdown to build the excitement
- Optional reminders to log deeds each evening, plus countdown messages at 30 days, 2 weeks, 1 week and Christmas Eve
- Parent PIN lock so only a grown-up can add children or edit deeds
- Private by design: everything stays on your phone, with no accounts, no ads and no tracking

Perfect for building good habits and holiday fun. Please note this is a just-for-fun family game from a parent, not from the real Santa (he's very busy).
